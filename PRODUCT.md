# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Independent sellers of visually distinctive, made-by-hand or made-to-order goods — 3D printing professionals, jewelers, painters, ceramicists, and sellers of cars, real estate, toys, and fashion. They are not running a storefront as their full-time job; they want a digital vitrine they can set up quickly and manage themselves, without technical help, alongside their actual craft. Their customers are visitors who land on `vitrine3d.com/{slug}`, browse a catalog, and want to ask about a specific piece without friction.

## Product Purpose

Vitrine3D lets any seller of visual goods stand up their own branded storefront (`vitrine3d.com/{slug}`) and manage its catalog from an admin panel, without needing a developer or a generic marketplace listing that buries their work next to competitors. Success is a seller receiving qualified quote requests routed straight to WhatsApp — the entire value loop (`ProductModal` → `buildWhatsAppUrl` → seller's phone) with no intermediary, no commission, and no marketplace algorithm between the seller and the buyer.

## Positioning

A personal boutique gallery for anyone who sells something worth looking at closely — not a marketplace listing, not a generic SaaS dashboard.

## Brand Personality

Elegant, airy, approachable. The interface should feel like walking into a boutique gallery with linen walls: quiet, warm, unhurried — the seller's product is the protagonist, the interface is the support, not the spectacle. Confidence comes from restraint, not decoration.

## Anti-references

Not a cold SaaS dashboard (sterile grays, dense data tables as the default surface, enterprise-tool chrome). Not a generic frozen marketplace grid (Amazon/Etsy-style anonymous product tiles that make every seller look interchangeable). Both flatten what should feel like a curated, personal space.

## Design Principles

- **The product is the protagonist.** Every surface — cards, modals, admin forms — exists to make the seller's work look good, not to compete with it visually.
- **One vitrine, many crafts.** Nothing in the interface should read as specific to 3D printing (or any single vertical); components and copy must generalize to jewelry, paintings, ceramics, cars, and beyond.
- **Elegance through restraint, not decoration.** Warmth and quality are carried by typography, spacing, and material choices (linen canvas, artisan gold accent, serif display type) — not gradients, heavy shadows, or ornamental chrome.
- **Zero friction to inquiry.** The path from browsing a product to sending a WhatsApp quote request must stay the shortest, clearest interaction on any page.
- **Self-serve simplicity.** Sellers manage their own storefront without technical help; admin flows should read as approachable, not like enterprise tooling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large/bold text ≥3:1, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all transitions and reveal animations.
